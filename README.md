# AI Automation Demos

Interactive walkthroughs of ten automation systems for local service businesses: missed-call text-back, review requests, appointment reminders, estimate follow-up, customer re-engagement, lead follow-up, cold outreach, an AI voice receptionist, client onboarding, and AI customer support.

Open `index.html` to browse them all. Each demo shows what the customer sees, what the system does behind the scenes, when a person takes over, how it's built, and a sample weekly report.

All businesses, people, phone numbers (555-01xx) and report figures are fictional sample data, not client results.
